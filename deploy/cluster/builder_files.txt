# The files a cluster needs to measure and build a cut photo, its homology table and the
# self-alignment scores of its queries, the embedding and lexicon banks of the photos and a
# cut's segment families, and the job scripts that run them: the import closure inside
# protea/ of protea.core.cut_photo.measure and .build and of protea.core.homology.build and
# .self_scores (the standard library, pyarrow and a pinned MMseqs2 binary are all they
# need), of protea.core.embedding_bank.build (which also needs numpy, and torch with the
# protea-backends plugins in the cluster's own environment), of
# protea.core.lexicon_bank.build (numpy, torch and Triton) and of
# protea.core.segment_families.build (pyarrow, numpy and a pinned MMseqs2 binary).
# send_builder_code.sh ships exactly these, and tests/test_cluster_builder_files.py fails
# when the closure and the protea/ lines disagree, or when a job script here is missing.
